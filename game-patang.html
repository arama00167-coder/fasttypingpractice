How to Play

  • A rival kite flies in carrying a word. Type the word fully and correctly before the kite reaches you.
  • Cut it and you hear the Basant cry — Wo Kaata! — plus points for every letter.
  • Run out of time and the rival cuts one of your 3 strings. Lose all 3 and the sky goes quiet.
  • One spelling mistake? No problem — the word restarts, but the kite keeps coming…

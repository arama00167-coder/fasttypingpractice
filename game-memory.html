Why This Game Makes You Faster

Fast typists do not read letter by letter — they glance at a whole word and type it in one flow. The Yaad Wala Game trains exactly that: look once, hold the word in memory, and let your fingers do the rest. Play it daily and your reading-ahead speed in normal tests will jump too.

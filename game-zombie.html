How to Play

  • Zombies walk in from the right, each carrying a word. Start typing a zombie's word — your defender locks onto it.
  • Finish the word correctly and the zombie falls in a burst of letters — points for every letter, plus a bonus for longer words.
  • If a zombie crosses the dashed line and reaches your base, you lose one of your 3 lives. Stop the closest zombie first!
  • Every 25 seconds a new wave arrives: zombies walk faster, appear more often, and carry longer words.

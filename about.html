About Fast Typing Practice

Fast Typing Practice is a free website that helps anyone learn to type faster — students, job seekers, office workers and anyone who simply wants quicker fingers.

Everything here is built around one idea: typing tests exist in every language, so practice should too. That is why our tests, lessons and Job Test Mode run in English, Urdu, Hindi, Arabic and Spanish, with instant WPM and accuracy results and no sign-up of any kind.

Alongside the serious practice you will find three typing games — the Space Shooter, Zombie Hamla and the Yaad Wala memory game — because the fastest way to build a habit is to make it fun.

The site is free and always will be for tests, lessons and games. Your results live only in your own browser; we have no accounts and collect no personal typing data.

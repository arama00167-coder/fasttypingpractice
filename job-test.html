Job Test Tips

📚 Preparation Tips

  • Fix accuracy before speed — aim for 95% or higher; mistakes cost real marks.
  • Practise 10–15 minutes every day. Daily beats one long weekly session.
  • Find your weakest keys (our practice report shows them) and drill those.
  • Do not look at the keyboard — eyes on the screen builds real speed.
  • In the last week, rehearse in Job Test Mode at your exact target speed.

🗓️ Test-Day Tips

  • Warm up for 2 minutes before the test if you can.
  • Start steady for the first 10 seconds, then settle into your rhythm.
  • Made a mistake? Do NOT stop — keep going; stopping wastes more time.
  • Sit straight, breathe slowly, keep fingers resting on the home row.
  • Use your time fully — a calm finish beats a rushed one.

Typical Speeds Employers Ask For

Clerk / LDC / office assistant posts commonly ask for about 30–35 WPM. Data entry roles usually want 35–45 WPM. Stenographers need 60–80+ WPM shorthand-side speed. Requirements vary by country and post, so always check your job advertisement — then set that number as your target above and practise until PASS feels easy.

Your 7-Day Practice Plan

  • Day 1–2: Home row lessons (Level 1) — 10 minutes, slow and accurate.
  • Day 3–4: Words and sentences (Levels 4–6) plus one 1-minute test.
  • Day 5: Weak-key practice on the Practice page — fix your top 3 problem keys.
  • Day 6: Two Job Test Mode attempts at your target. Note the gap.
  • Day 7: Full rehearsal: Job Test Mode, test-day routine, start to finish.

Repeat the week until PASS shows up twice in a row — then you are genuinely ready.

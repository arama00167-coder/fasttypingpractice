Space Shooter

Enemy ships dive at you in Galaxy-style formation waves, and every ship carries a word. Locked on, your fighter pours a stream of bolts at the target and fires its laser with every correct letter — finish a ship's word and the ship blasts apart in a burst of letters. If a ship lands, you lose a life — you have 3. Waves get faster and words get longer the longer you survive!

How to Play

  • Start typing any ship's word — your ship locks onto it and follows it across the sky.
  • Enemy ships attack in formation waves that sway side to side as they dive. While you are locked on, your ship streams bolts at the target — and every correct letter fires the big laser.
  • Finish the word correctly and the ship explodes: points for every letter, plus a bonus for longer words. Clear the whole wave and the next wave arrives faster, with longer words.
  • A ship that reaches the bottom costs you one of your 3 lives. Always shoot the lowest ship first!

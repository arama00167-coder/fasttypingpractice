How to Sit and Place Your Fingers

  • Sit straight, feet flat, screen at eye level — elbows relaxed at your sides.
  • Rest your fingers on the home row: left hand A S D F, right hand J K L ; — feel the small bumps on F and J.
  • Keep your eyes on the screen. Looking down is the number one speed killer.
  • Slow is smooth, and smooth becomes fast. Accuracy first — always.

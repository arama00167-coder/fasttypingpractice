Why Untimed Practice Works

Racing a clock too early teaches your fingers to panic. Untimed practice lets you build the two things that create real speed: correct finger habits and accuracy. Type slowly enough that almost every letter is right; the speed arrives by itself over the following weeks — and the weak-keys report above tells you exactly where to aim next.

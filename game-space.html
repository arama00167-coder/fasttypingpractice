Space Shooter

Enemy ships are dropping out of the sky, and every ship carries a word. Your fighter ship fires its laser with every correct letter — finish a ship's word and the ship blasts apart in a burst of letters. If a ship lands, you lose a life — you have 3. Ships get faster and words get longer the longer you survive!

How to Play

  • Start typing any ship's word — your ship locks onto it and follows it across the sky.
  • Finish the word correctly and the ship explodes: points for every letter, plus a bonus for longer words.
  • A ship that reaches the bottom costs you one of your 3 lives. Always shoot the lowest ship first!
  • Every 25 seconds the level rises: ships fall faster, appear more often, and carry longer words.
